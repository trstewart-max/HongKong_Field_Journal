A scrollytelling website combining real survey data, foot-traffic estimates, and personal photography to explore twelve of Hong Kong's most visited attractions. Built for ARTS1007, a class I took while studying abroad at CUHK.

View live site --> https://trstewart-max.github.io/HongKong_Field_Journal/

What this is

Twelve stops around Hong Kong, each paired with:

A personal photo and written reflection
My own rating vs. the class average, on both enjoyment and cultural significance
Estimated annual foot traffic, sourced from official filings where available and clearly flagged as modeled estimates elsewhere

The site also includes:

A breakdown of who answered the class survey (30 ARTS1007 students, by origin: local, Mainland China, long-haul, other Asia)
A citywide foot-traffic overview across all twelve stops
A scatter plot testing whether higher foot traffic actually correlates with higher cultural significance ratings
A closing comparison chart ranking every stop by how far my score diverged from the class's
Data sources
Survey data: a 30-response Google Form survey of ARTS1007 classmates, rating each attraction on enjoyment and cultural/heritage significance
Foot traffic: official visitor figures where published (Hong Kong Disneyland, Ocean Park, the Peak Tram, Tai Kwun), proxy metrics where no direct figure exists (Star Ferry ridership for Victoria Harbour, venue capacity for horse racing), and modeled estimates elsewhere, all labeled in the chart legend
